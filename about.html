About

A small note behind this space.

This project was first inspired by a book I once read — It's Not Always Depression.

It helped me begin to see emotions differently — not as something to fix, but as something already doing their job.

Emotions are not enemies. They are often the first response our body has to the world.

When we begin to notice them, to listen a little more closely, or simply stay with them instead of against them, things can feel more open, and a little more like our own.

This space is a small attempt to stay with that idea. It is not meant to give answers. It is simply here to hold a moment with you.
